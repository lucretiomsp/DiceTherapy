Keys: Space play/stop · D roll · R regenerate

Notes. Each note is weighted by its consonance with the chord, the strength of its step (root, third and fifth on the beat), stepwise voice leading and its interval against the bass. Every bar of the pattern gets a chord; 64 and 128 steps repeat a 4-bar progression.

Lengths. The pattern (16 to 128 steps) restarts every lane when it ends. Each lane loops on its own length, 3 to 64 steps, so lanes can drift against each other; a note that repeats over several chords is chosen to fit all of them. Lanes that match the pattern length follow it when you change it.

Durations. Each note lasts a number of steps, at most until the next note of its lane, so lanes stay monophonic. Tap a step to switch it. Drag a note to the right to hold it longer, drag back to shorten it, or use Shift + arrow keys. Tap a held step to start a new note there. Gate is the share of the length that sounds. Both export to Coypu as durations: and gateTimes:.

Notation. C4 = MIDI 60. ↑ ↓ raise or lower a quarter tone. Partch notes show cents from 12-TET.

MIDI. Saw, Square and Tri go out on channels 1, 2 and 3; muted lanes send nothing, the kick stays local. Microtones are sent as pitch bend, with the bend range set to ±2 semitones by RPN. Needs Chrome, Edge or Firefox over HTTPS; Safari and iOS have no Web MIDI.

Sources. Scales: SuperCollider Scale.directory. Rhythms: the world rhythms of Coypu, random ones follow randomTrigsWithProbability:.
